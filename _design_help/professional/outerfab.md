**[Outerfab](https://outerfab.com/)** is based in Shenzhen and coordinates the work between bare silicon and a usable prototype or small batch. For GF180MCU projects, including die from wafer.space, it offers PCB design and review, chip-on-board bonding, and electrical checks of bonded connections.

### Services

- **PCB design and review:** Breakout boards, bonding diagrams, pad maps and panelisation for chip-on-board assembly.
- **Chip-on-board bonding:** Die attach, aluminium wedge or gold wire bonding, and hard epoxy encapsulation.
- **Electrical testing:** Checks for open connections and shorts on agreed pads, with test coverage and reporting defined for each job.
- **Wafer dicing and division:** Backgrinding, dicing and sorting into waffle packs, arranged through partners in Asia.
- **Packing and shipping:** Kitting, packing and fulfilment for small batches.

Outerfab works with engineers and manufacturing partners. Send the die dimensions, pad map, board design if available, quantity and testing needs to discuss the job.
