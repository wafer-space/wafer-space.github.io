**[Partcl](https://partcl.com/)** builds Boson, a GPU-native digital
implementation engine covering synthesis, placement, clock tree synthesis,
and static timing analysis. By running the entire flow on GPUs, Boson turns
full-chip iterations that traditionally take hours into minutes.

For GF180MCU shuttle projects, that speed changes how you design:

- **Explore before you commit**: run many architecture, floorplan, and
  constraint experiments before the shuttle deadline instead of one or two
- **Timing confidence before tapeout**: fast timing analysis with parasitics
  lets you verify your design closes timing before committing to a shuttle slot
- **Works alongside your existing flow**: use Boson for rapid QoR and timing
  exploration while signing off with the open-source flow you already trust

Partcl's team combines EDA and GPU-computing backgrounds, and offers direct
support for teams pushing timing or turnaround limits on their designs.
