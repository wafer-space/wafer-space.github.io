What designers actually achieved

Logic cells per mm² of core, in every measured design

A design's core is the area inside the pad ring where its logic is placed: 12.92 mm² of a full slot's 19.65 mm². Dividing by the core shows how tightly a design was packed. Dividing by the whole slot inside its seal ring shows how much logic the slot gave the customer.
