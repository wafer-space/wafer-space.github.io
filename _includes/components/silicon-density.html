What fits on 1 mm² of GF180MCU silicon?

The theoretical maximum, then what designers achieved

The maximum packs one kind of cell edge to edge, with no room for wiring, using the densest library for that cell. No real design gets close, because the cells must be wired together. What designers achieved is measured from the layouts of {{ a.designs }} designs made on GF180MCU.

What designers actually achieved

Logic cells per mm² of core, in every measured design

A design's core is the area inside the pad ring where its logic is placed: 12.92 mm² of a full slot's 19.65 mm². Dividing by the core shows how tightly a design was packed. Dividing by the whole slot inside its seal ring shows how much logic the slot gave the customer.
