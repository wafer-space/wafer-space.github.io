## How Die Sorting Works

After the wafers are cut into individual dies, each die needs to be picked up, inspected, and placed into a tape-and-reel
for shipping. This is handled by the sorting robot which works through the diced wafer one die at a time.

Note the white layer of material under the wafer - this is tape. It holds the wafer together and the dies in place,
since the wafer loses all rigidity after the dicing.
