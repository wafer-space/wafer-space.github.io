Hey folks,

It's that time again -- Run 3 is open for submission.

## What's new?

We're now able to offer 1,000 dies for as little as $2,000 -- that's $2/die. This is achievable thanks to our newest
slot offering: the 0.5x0.5 quarter slot. This tiny slot packs a punch with 4.9 mm² of silicon allowing you to tapeout test structures, analog circuits, or compact digital designs without paying for silicon you do not need.

{% include image.html file="/assets/images/slots/0p5x0p5_gelpack.jpg" description="Quarter slot overlaid on a full
slot die for size comparison" %}

Detailed comparisons between the different slot sizes can be seen at [mithro.github.io/gf180mcu-project-template/](https://mithro.github.io/gf180mcu-project-template/). If you're not sure what size would suit you best, don't hesitate to reach
out to us or our community - see the [Get Involved](#get-involved) section.

Behind the scenes, there have been various toolchain improvements and bugfixes to make the processes a lot smoother. The
submission platform now catches more issues, earlier and faster. Stricter DRCs allow you to be confident in your tapeout.

With Run 2 closed, there's another batch of open source designs available for you to study, adapt and build upon for your
own tapeout. Projects vary from simple test structures to Linux-capable SoCs, covering digital, analog and mixed-signal
approaches.
